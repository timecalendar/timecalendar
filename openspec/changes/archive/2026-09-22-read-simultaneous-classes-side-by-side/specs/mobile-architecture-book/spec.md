## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The Architecture Book records the T11 overlap and disambiguation contracts

Current-state Calendar and testing guidance SHALL describe stable identity-backed positive-interval packing, adjacency as non-overlap, minimum equal-width non-covering columns, complete pre-clipping cluster calculation, stable placement through scroll and zoom, and explicit accessible target-conflict choice without hidden events or count thresholds. It SHALL identify the focused property/component/contract proof and SHALL distinguish fabricated density/frame/node evidence from later supported-workload calibration and native-device acceptance. `CHANGELOG.md` SHALL record the refinement without adding a new ADR or architecture boundary.

#### Scenario: Calendar guidance describes stable overlap behavior

- **WHEN** the Calendar Architecture Book page is read after T11
- **THEN** it explains total start/end/stable-identity ordering, adjacency, complete clusters, equal-width columns, and scroll/zoom stability
- **AND** it preserves the existing original-identity, complete-page, native-owner, and stored-fact contracts

#### Scenario: Accessibility guidance describes explicit conflict choice

- **WHEN** effective minimum-size targets cannot remain distinct
- **THEN** current guidance requires one localized accessible chooser path with every involved event available exactly once
- **AND** it forbids silent z-order selection, hidden events, arbitrary density thresholds, and duplicate semantic nodes

#### Scenario: Testing guidance bounds the evidence

- **WHEN** the T11 proof guidance and changelog are inspected
- **THEN** they identify permutation/property, component interaction/accessibility, repository-contract, fabricated density/frame/node, and local-green evidence
- **AND** they leave final workload calibration and physical-device accessibility acceptance to their planned later slices
